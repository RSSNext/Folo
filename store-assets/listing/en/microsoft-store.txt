Folo is the AI RSS reader that brings everything you follow into one calm timeline, then helps you read it.

Follow blogs, news sites, newsletters, podcasts, video channels and social feeds in one place. Ask AI about what you read, get a digest of your whole timeline, and let summaries and translations do the heavy lifting.

AI THAT READS WITH YOU
• Chat with AI about an article, a feed, a folder or your whole timeline
• Summarize your timeline into a quick digest of what matters
• AI tasks: schedule daily or weekly briefings, delivered to chat or email
• AI summaries and translation in English, Chinese, Japanese or French, shown side by side with the original or on their own
• Text-to-speech with natural voices
• Connect MCP services, choose your AI model, or bring your own API key

ONE TIMELINE FOR EVERYTHING YOU FOLLOW
• Subscribe to any RSS, Atom or JSON feed, or search for sources by name
• Follow sites without a feed through thousands of RSSHub routes
• Import and export OPML to move from any other reader
• Articles, social posts, pictures, videos and podcasts, each with its own layout
• Folders, stars, lists and an inbox with its own email address for newsletters

BUILT FOR THE DESK
• A three-pane layout designed for fast, focused reading
• Customizable keyboard shortcuts and a command menu
• Actions: filter, star, rewrite, notify or summarize new entries with your own rules
• Send articles to Obsidian, Readwise, Instapaper, Zotero, Cubox, Outline, Readeck and Eagle
• Themes, reading fonts, custom CSS and code highlighting
• Spotlight highlights the keywords you care about

EVERYWHERE YOU READ
Subscriptions, read status and stars sync across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone, iPad and the web.

OPEN SOURCE, NO ADS
Folo is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license and has no ads. It is built in the open with a community of readers and developers.

PLANS
Folo is free to use with a free account. Basic, Plus and Pro plans add more subscriptions, higher AI limits and automations such as AI tasks, inboxes and bring-your-own-key.
