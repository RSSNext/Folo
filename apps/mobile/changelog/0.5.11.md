# What's New in v0.5.11

## Improvements

- Text-to-speech starts reliably on long articles, and stopping it or switching articles no longer leaves audio playing in the background
- The player bar now also shows on article and folder screens on iPad, Android and iOS before 26, so text-to-speech can be paused or stopped there
- Settings sync covers every appearance, general and AI setting again; only device-specific ones such as the interface language stay on each device
- The action language now syncs, so AI chat answers in the language you picked
- Finished the Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and French translations, including text that was previously hard-coded in English
- Adopted the scene-based app life cycle for iOS 27
- Updated to the latest Expo SDK 57 and React Native Firebase releases

## No longer broken

- Fixed Google and other social sign-ins hanging when the sign-in failed; the app now closes the sign-in sheet and shows an error
- Fixed the tab bar turning transparent and drawing its icons over the content on iPad and iOS before 26
- Fixed custom subscription titles not showing on entries
- Fixed article images that failed to load on Android; they now retry through the image proxy
- Fixed unread counts that stayed behind after reading new entries
- Fixed English title case being applied to non-English titles and labels
- Fixed only device-specific settings syncing between devices
